FocusiQ – what to expect

Soon you’ll be invited to take a short FocusiQ assessment. It’s not an exam and it’s not pass or fail. There’s nothing to revise and there are no trick questions. It shows how you approach everyday work, so we can support you and improve how we work together.

≈ 30 minutes for the assessment + 5 minutes to read and confirm first 7 short sections, four timed Calculator allowed Not pass/fail

1Why we’re doing it

  • To understand how each of us likes to work – how we take in information, prioritise, decide and follow things through.
  • To support people better: clearer instructions, the right tools and checklists, and training where it would really help.
  • To spot things in our processes that make work harder for many people – unclear priorities, for example – and fix them.
  • Different people work in different ways. There’s no single “right” profile.

2What it involves

  • Realistic scenarios from our customers – hotels, holiday parks, housekeepers and purchasing managers: a quote to check, a busy morning to plan.
  • Picture puzzles, code-breaking clues and sales figures (targets, pipeline, margin). Some are meant to be hard – nobody is expected to get everything.
  • Four sections are timed and show a clock. The others have no time limit. Bring a calculator if you like one.
  • The last section asks what motivates you at work. There are no right or wrong answers there.
  • FocusiQ also notes how you work through it (time taken, changed answers), as explained in the privacy notice.

3Before your session

  • You’ll get an email from “Walter Geering”. Sign in with your usual Walter Geering account – the same as for the Hub.
  • Read the short privacy notice and confirm a few details. This takes about 5 minutes and you can do it in advance.
  • Need an adjustment? Extra time, larger text, a screen reader, breaks or a quiet room – just ask on the form. You don’t need to share medical details, and it never counts against you.

4On the day

  • Find somewhere quiet, ideally on a computer or laptop that’s charged. Bring your glasses or anything you normally use.
  • Work as you normally would – don’t rush and don’t overthink.
  • Do it on your own, and please don’t discuss the questions with colleagues.
  • Your answers save as you go. If something goes wrong, stop and use “Questions or concerns” – nothing is lost.

5Afterwards

  • You’ll see a thank-you screen and get a short email confirming it’s complete.
  • A Director reviews the results with you, as a starting point for a conversation.
  • You’ll receive your own written summary in FocusiQ.

6Questions?

  • Use “Questions or concerns” in FocusiQ at any time – before, during or after.
  • You can also ask for a copy of your information, ask for a correction, or object to how it’s used.
  • Or speak to your Director.

Our promise: your results are confidential and used only within Walter Geering. Directors see your full report; you receive your own summary. Results are never published, never shown to colleagues, and never used on their own to make decisions about your employment.
